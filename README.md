# World News — Monday, 23 November 2026

A one-image summary of the world's news of Monday 23 November 2026, built for make.fun's [Sum up 23 November's world news in one image](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261123) bounty (`news-image-20261123`).

`main.ts` (Node 22) copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`, after checking the PNG signature and the 1 MiB size limit. The image is a newspaper-style front page, 1024×1024, drawn as HTML/CSS and rendered in headless Chrome; the lead illustration (the Russian corvette Merkury burning off Benghazi) was generated with an image model, and every word of text is set in the page itself so it reads exactly.

4. **Gulf** — Iran claims 78 U.S. drones shot down, 58 Reapers and 20 Predators; the United States has not confirmed the figures. (PressTV, Tasnim)
5. **Yemen** — Houthis claim a strike on the Rabigh refinery on Saudi Arabia's Red Sea coast; damage is unconfirmed, and Saudi jets struck the port of Hodeida. (Reuters via TBS News)
6. **Also today** — Nepal PM Balen Shah welcomed in New Delhi, meets Modi; India holds third place in the Asia Power Index; the UN condemns Khartoum drone strikes, Sudan blames Ethiopia; the U.S. Army to detonate unstable explosives in Iowa despite a court order. (Inshorts, Addis Standard)

All stories happened or were first reported on 23 November 2026. Sources checked on 23 November 2026: Reuters, AP, CNN, The Times, Fox News, TBS News, PressTV, Tasnim, Sports Illustrated.
